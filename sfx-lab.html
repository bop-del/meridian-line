SOUND LAB

Audition every sound effect. Sounds go through the same gain, compressor and limiter as the game. "Rapid x8" repeats a sound at a realistic fire rate and obeys the same voice limits as the game (gap and max voices). "Prev" plays the previous version (for the player lasers the first laser recipe, for the enemy, turret and boss sounds the sound the game used at that call site before), "v1" the very first player laser, "Hold 4 s" fires continuously at the game rate and "Compare" plays v1, prev and new three shots each, and "Family" plays player L1, L2, L3, enemy, turret, allies, boss charge, cannon and beam in a row. The green numbers under a sound are measured on the shot you just heard (peak, RMS, A-weighted energy as a loudness figure, spectral centroid, band shares, fall in octaves). Pitch variation adds a random pitch offset per shot on top of what each recipe already randomises.
